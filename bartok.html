Bartók's Info

Overview
  • Refill his water bowl
  • Make sure his food dispenser has a blue light
  • In the evenings, give him Zyrtec with peanut butter
  • Check that nothing is obstructing the doggie door
  • Glance at the yard / around the first floor for any safety issues
Water

Bartók's water bowl is the red bowl to the left of the automatic food dispenser in the white cabinet in the left rear corner of the house.

You can either carry it to the sink to refill it or use a container (like the large green jug) to bring water to it.

If it's half full or more, you can just top it off.

If it's close to empty and you bring it to the sink, toss out the last drops and give it a rinse before refilling.

Food

Bartók's automatic food dispenser is in the white cabinet in the left rear corner of the house.

The light should be blue, which indicates it has food inside and is not blocked from dispensing food.

If the light is red and there is kibble in the tray, please verbally encourage Bartók to eat his food. It drops in two stages, so the red light in this case probably means the second drop was blocked by him not eating the first.

If the light is red and there is no kibble in the tray, the machine is (nearly) out of food. Please refill it from the bag of food in the plastic wheeled container to the right of the white cabinet. There is a little knob atop the machine to allow you to unlatch and relatch the lid.

Medicine

Every evening, Bartók should get one Zyrtec pill with some peanut butter.

If we forgot to leave out a spoon for this, they are in the drawer of the left of the sink.

Bartók is very good about sitting for his pill and will politely lick up all the peanut butter and the pill. Watch carefully so you'll notice if the pill falls on the floor. You may need to rotate the spoon to an angle where his tongue can more easily scoop up the pill.
